About Us

Who we are

Seph Tech and Health is a health and nutrition company operating under SEYCO. We manufacture nutritious food products, promote healthy living, support disease prevention, and work with communities to fight malnutrition, non-communicable diseases, and food insecurity.

Vision

To become Africa's leading provider of innovative nutrition and community health solutions that improve lives and create healthier communities.

Mission

To develop and provide affordable, high-quality nutritional products and health solutions while empowering communities through education, innovation, partnerships, and sustainable development.
